# Photography credits

All images in this directory are **Goalkeep's own photography**, supplied by the
team in September 2026 (Drive → *Photos for Mohith*). They show real Goalkeep
workshops, real partner teams and real programme sites, so they may be captioned
as such.

The four Unsplash stand-ins that previously lived here (`field-community`,
`field-group`, `field-health`, `field-meeting`) have been deleted — they were
contextual stock and were never Goalkeep's clients.
