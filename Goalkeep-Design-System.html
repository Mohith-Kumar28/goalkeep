Colour rules, enforced by pnpm check:design

1. No grey text. Text is ink or white; anything softer is an alpha of one of those two.
2. Navy and white are the grounds. Tints are occasional highlight bands, not a third ground.
3. Gold is a marker, never text on a light ground (hard-banned).
4. Pop teal and coral are display colours on white. On navy use -lift; pop versions under 24px are banned.
5. Any opaque colour not in the token list fails check-palette.mjs. No stray Tailwind defaults.
6. Focus: blue 3px halo on light, gold halo on navy. Text selection is gold behind ink.

The headline pattern

India's social sector isn't short on data. It's short on decisions made with data.

Headings are set light. Only the phrase that carries the sentence is bold italic (<em> renders as italic 800). Emphasis is never a colour and never a second, lighter line.

Copy formatting

  • Sentence case everywhere: headings, buttons, nav. Eyebrows are written lowercase in content and uppercased by CSS.
  • Line lengths: hero lead 46ch, headings 22–36ch. Headings use text-wrap: balance.
  • Hyphenated words never break across lines.
  • Paragraph body on any light ground is ink. "Just keep it in black."

05 · Patterns & brand devices

The hand, the highlighter and the arcs of the mark.

The highlighter · .marker

turn data into decisions

we design, build and enable
  1. Chisel ends: CSS skewX(±4°).
  2. Wobbling edges: an SVG mask on a ::before.
  3. Uneven ink: darker ends, ink settling at the bottom.
  4. A degree off level: rotate(−0.4°) or 0.3° (.marker-b).

The stroke overshoots the word. Use white with navy text on dark grounds, and split wrapped prose into one stroke per line.

Hand-drawn marks · <Scribble>

Circle, oval, ring, underline (×4 kinds), squiggle, arrows, bracket, star, spiral, check, zigzag, cross.

  • Always --gk-scribble yellow. Round caps, 4–7px weight.
  • Deliberately imperfect: circles don't close, underlines wobble.
  • Draw in once on scroll (0.9s). Margin notes sit at −3° with an arrow.

Logo-arc shapes · <ArcPiece>, <ShapeField>

Example
Large, anchored to a corner, bleeding off the edge.
  • Every shape is an arc of the logo ring. Sweep 360 is the ring, 180 the half, ~80–100 the "comma". Nothing generic.
  • Large (200–440px), stroke tuned to about 48px, anchored to a corner and running off it, never over text.
  • The visible part must show curvature and at least one round cap, or it reads as a blob.
  • At most one coloured piece per light band; two tonal navy-lift pieces on the hero.
  • Drifts gently against scroll. Hidden on phones.
  • Closing band: the mark's five arcs start scattered and assemble on scroll. This is the only place the whole mark is built from its parts.

Photography & light

  • Goalkeep's own photography only (see public/photos/CREDITS.md).
  • Never framed in a box. Photos dissolve into the ground with five-stop mask fades (.dissolve-r / -l / -b / -rb / -soft, .photo-bleed).
  • Fade the photograph only, never drawn illustration. Card images run flush to the card edge.
  • Partner logos are greyscale and take colour on hover, in a ticker that fades out 210px from each side.
  • Hero only: a faint pointer glow and a rim light on the case card. Light only; nothing moves, scales or tilts.
